MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Corp
("Customer") and Vendor Cloud Ltd ("Provider").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means the cloud data-processing services described in each Order Form.
1.2 "Personal Data" means information relating to an identified natural person.

3. Term and Renewal
3.1 This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods
unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days
before the end of the then-current term.

4. Fees
4.1 Fees are $20,000 per month, invoiced monthly in advance.
4.2 Late payments accrue a penalty of five percent (5%) per month on the overdue balance.

6. Data Protection
6.1 Provider may process Customer Personal Data to deliver the Services. The parties
have not executed a separate Data Processing Agreement, and data may be processed in
any region at Provider's discretion.

8. Limitation of Liability
8.1 Customer shall indemnify Provider for any breach of this Agreement, including any
data breach, without limitation.
8.2 Neither party's liability shall be limited under this Agreement, and consequential
damages are recoverable in full.

10. Governing Law
10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
